Medical Atlas Navigation Guide

• Orbit Camera: Left-click and drag to rotate around the human body. Right-click and drag to pan up, down, left, or right. Scroll to zoom in and out.

• First-Person Mode: Use W / S to walk forward/backward, A / D to strafe left/right, and move your mouse to look around in 360°.

• Layer Isolation: Use the left sidebar to isolate the Skeletal System, Muscular System, Visceral Organs, or Arteries and Veins.

• Exploded View: Drag the radial separation slider at the bottom-left to expand all anatomical layers outwards.

• Trilingual Atlas: Click EN, हिन्दी, or বাংলা to switch languages instantly across all medical descriptions.
