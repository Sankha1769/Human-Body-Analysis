CIRCULATORY SYSTEM

Heart

Thoracic cavity · slightly left of centre

The heart is a muscular organ that works as the body's pump, moving blood through the lungs and around the rest of the body.

Primary function

It keeps blood circulating, delivering oxygen and nutrients to tissues and carrying away waste products.

Did you know?

  • Your heart beats around 100,000 times in a typical day.
  • It has four chambers that work in a coordinated rhythm.

Credits & licences

Anatomy data: BodyParts3D 4.0 (adult male reference anatomy), © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed CC BY 4.0. Dataset: dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp. Mitsuhashi et al. (2009), doi:10.1093/nar/gkn613.

Adaptations (made by this project, not by the data authors): meshes were obtained from the open-source Human Atlas project (MIT, ashemag/human-atlas), where they were converted from millimetres/Z-up to metres/Y-up and simplified with meshoptimizer (0.2% relative error). Here they were repacked into layer GLB files with quantised normals (this build ships all seven layers). Colours, grouping into systems and organ mapping are ours. Structures are therefore simplified compared with the original.

Known gaps: the source data has no lung surface and only part of the liver (caudate lobe, ducts, vessels). It is a single male reference body with no skin texture.

Female model (partial): Visible Human Female reference organs from the HuBMAP CCF 3D Reference Object Library v1.0 (CC BY 4.0; source data: U.S. National Library of Medicine Visible Human Project; large intestine by Stony Brook University, lymph nodes by NIH). Included: skin, heart, lungs and airways, kidneys, spleen, thymus, large intestine, lymph nodes, pelvic bones and blood vessels. Not included: brain, skeleton beyond the pelvis, muscles, nerves, liver, stomach, pancreas, small intestine, bladder and all reproductive organs. The Allen Institute brain model was excluded because it has a different licence. Models were repacked into layer files by this project (baked transforms, duplicate vessels removed, quantised normals).

Software: Three.js (MIT) — OrbitControls, GLTFLoader, RoomEnvironment.
